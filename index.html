Our Story

A Legacy of Flavour

For nearly four decades, Hotel Rahmath has been Kozhikode's most beloved dining destination — a place where the rich culinary heritage of Malabar is honoured in every dish. Our chefs craft each recipe with hand-picked spices, time-tested techniques, and a deep reverence for the flavours that define our culture.

From the fragrant Thalassery Biriyani to succulent Tandoori specialities, every meal is an invitation to experience food as it was meant to be — generous, soulful, and shared with the ones you love.
